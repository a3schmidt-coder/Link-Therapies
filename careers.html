Join our team!

Email admin@linktherapies.com if you are interested in a career or internship at Link.

At Link, we believe the key to delivering quality services is a supportive environment. Realistic caseload sizes and adequate time for session preparation are critical elements to quality client care. Our goal is to create an environment where our team members love showing up to work.

Our mission is to lead the industry in providing interdisciplinary care to our clients. We strive for excellence, and delivering high quality services is of the utmost importance to us. We are looking for driven team members who value collaboration and are committed to learning. We are excited to hire individuals who share our vision and are eager to grow as part of our team.

Why choose us?

  • Competitive compensation
  • Limited caseload size to ensure quality care
  • Flexible caseload models dependent on candidate clinical and supervisory interests
  • Supportive clinical supervision and mentorship
  • Professional development
  • Health and other benefits such as 401k, funds for client materials, PTO, and more!
