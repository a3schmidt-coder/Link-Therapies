Our Mission Speech Team OT Team ABA Team

We provide the tools necessary to support our clients in navigating their world and becoming the most independent and happy version of themselves. We believe that it is critical for everyone in the individual’s environment to have a deep understanding of the biological, psychological, and social variables that may impact the individual in any given situation.

Our team will work together, when needed, to provide consistent and comprehensive support. Our clients can receive services from either or both divisions as determined by their treatment team.
